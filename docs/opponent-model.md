# Opponent model

Generated by `tools/make_opponent_doc.py` from the running code. Narrative and limitations are in `docs/stage-03-report.md`.

## How individuals are built

An archetype supplies the mean of each trait. Four latent factors are drawn per player (zL looseness, zA aggression, zS stickiness, zK skill) and load onto the traits, so related traits move together. `spread` scales how far one player strays from the archetype. Traits are clamped to the ranges below, and contradictory combinations are repaired (a passive player cannot bluff a lot, a very tight player does not chase draws, a sticky player does not overfold, a very aggressive player does check-raise, the river barrel never exceeds the turn barrel).

## Limited observation of the hero

Each opponent keeps Beta counts of what it saw the hero do (pre-flop play, pre-flop raises, post-flop aggression, folds to bets, hands shown at showdown). An event is noticed with probability `attn` (30% of that if the player already folded). Belief = (k x prior + successes) / (k + trials), with population priors `POP` in `index.html`; trials are capped so old evidence fades. How much a belief changes play = `adapt` x trials / (trials + 20). Nothing is learned from events the player did not see.
