Range Trainer

Three questions, two small tables. Practice them until they're automatic.

Your card: after the flop (tap to hide)
  1. What do they usually have?
    One bet on the flop
    Any pair or a draw
    A second bet
    Top pair
    A third bet, or any raise
    Two pair or better
    Do you have at least that? Yes: call. Two pair or better against a pair: raise.
  2. No? Count your outs.
    Flush draw
    9
    Straight draw, open both ends
    8
    Flush and straight draw
    15
    Straight draw with a gap
    4
    One pair, for two pair or trips
    5
    Pocket pair, for trips
    2
    Outs × 4 on the flop, × 2 on the turn = your chance.
  3. Is it more than the price?
    1/3 pot bet
    20%
    1/2 pot bet
    25%
    2/3 or 3/4 pot bet
    30%
    Full pot bet
    33%
    More: call. Less: fold.
Your card: before the flop (tap to hide)
  1. Limpers, nobody raised
    2+ limpers: small pairs (22 to 66), suited connectors (54s to JTs)
    Limp
    Anything you'd open from your seat
    Raise
    Everything else
    Fold
    Raise to 4bb + 1 per limper.
  2. One raise in front
    QQ+, AK
    3-bet
    77 to JJ, AQ, KQs
    Call
    AJs, KJs, QJs, JTs (not against an early raise)
    Call
    Small pairs 22 to 66, with 20× the raise behind
    Call
    Raise came from CO or BTN, and you're not in a blind: also AJ, KQ, T9s, 98s, 87s
    Call
    Everything else
    Fold
    3-bet to 3× their raise in position, 4× out of position.
  3. You raised, they re-raised
    AA, KK
    4-bet
    QQ, JJ, AK, AQs
    Call
    Smaller pairs, with 20× the re-raise behind
    Call
    Everything else
    Fold
  4. Another re-raise (a 4-bet)
    AA, KK
    All in
    Everything else
    Fold
